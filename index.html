Sources & Methodology

OXFORD
Frey & Osborne (2013, updated 2023) — "The Future of Employment" — foundational study estimating 47% of US jobs are at high risk of computerization. Uses task-based analysis across 702 occupations.
GOLDMAN
Goldman Sachs (2023) — "The Potentially Large Effects of AI on Economic Growth" — estimates 300M full-time jobs globally exposed to automation. Two-thirds of jobs partially automatable.
MCKINSEY
McKinsey Global Institute (2023) — "The Economic Potential of Generative AI" — generative AI could automate 60-70% of employee time across industries by 2030.
WEF
World Economic Forum — Future of Jobs Report 2025 — 85M jobs displaced by 2025, 97M new roles created. Net positive but requires massive reskilling.
US BLS
Bureau of Labor Statistics — Occupational Outlook Handbook — provides 10-year employment projections for all major occupations tied to specific college majors.
